02

Concept Drift in Hierarchical Federated Learning

Research Project

Federated systems must keep learning as client data shifts over time and clients drop in and out — most aggregation strategies aren't tested against that.

A PyTorch/CNN framework simulating concept drift and client dropout in hierarchical federated learning, comparing FedAvg, FedProx, FedNova and a hybrid aggregation strategy.

Key contribution Designed the experimental framework and drift/dropout simulation, and implemented and compared all four aggregation strategies for hierarchical networks.

02

DecideHer — AI Decision Executive

AI.WOMEN Hackathon 2026 · AI.Hamburg · Hamburg, Germany · Sep 12–13, 2026

Companies collect AI ideas one request at a time, with no shared context — so three teams asking for an "AI customer agent," an "AI customer-data assistant" and "AI history search" can look like three projects when they're really one fragmented problem.

A decision-support tool that reads AI and improvement ideas together, clusters them against company context, and runs weak evidence through a deterministic gate instead of letting AI auto-score and greenlight requests — built with a 5-person, all-women team in 48 hours.

Key contribution Built the core decision pipeline and its Gemini LLM integration — turning free-text idea submissions into structured root-cause and capability data, and generating the AI clarification questions.

07 — About

About

I am interested in building intelligent systems that can understand complex information, learn meaningful representations, and solve real-world problems. My work spans artificial intelligence, machine learning, knowledge graphs, multimodal learning, natural language processing and streaming applications, with experience applying these technologies across research and engineering projects.

I am particularly drawn to problems involving complex and heterogeneous data — from text, images, tables, and diagrams to sensor and domain-specific information — and exploring how AI can connect and reason across these different sources. Most recently, I have been working on intelligent systems for aircraft maintenance documentation at the German Aerospace Center (DLR).

My background combines applied research with hands-on engineering, and I enjoy taking ideas from experimentation to working systems. I am especially interested in developing AI that is reliable, interpretable, and useful in real-world applications.
